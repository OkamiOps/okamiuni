Common questions

Zoom sign-in does not come back to the app. The app listens on 127.0.0.1:53720 while you sign in. Make sure no firewall or other app blocks that port, then click Connect again.

Zoom does not appear as a meeting option. Connect Zoom first in Settings › Calendars › Meeting rooms. See the Zoom guide.

How do I remove the Zoom connection? Click Disconnect next to Zoom in Settings, and optionally remove OkamiUNI in the Zoom App Marketplace under Manage › Added Apps.
