# OkamiUNI 0.6.7 — As many meeting accounts as you want

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.7/docs/releases/v0.6.7.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 22, 2026. App version **0.6.7**, build **22**.

## What's new

- **As many Meet, Zoom, Teams and Zoho accounts as you want.** Settings › Calendars lists each one with its own **Disconnect**, and **Connect another account** stays under the list. On a new event, when more than one account can create the room, the **Account** row has **switch**. The room is created by the account you pick.
- **Removing a Meet account leaves the mailbox.** Meet uses the Google sign-in of that mailbox. Disconnecting it only takes it off the Meet list. Connecting another opens Google so you can choose the account. Teams asks which Microsoft account. Zoom and Zoho open their sign-in in the browser.
- **A Google mailbox brings its calendar.** The primary calendar shows up with the permission you already gave, so you do not have to sign in again to see it. The other calendars on that account show up the next time you sign in with Google. Events you create and delete there go to that Google calendar.
- **Sign in with Google finishes the sheet.** After Google or Zoho accepts the account, the add-account sheet closes. On a Google domain, with the password left empty, **Sign in with Google** is the button that continues.

## Installation

If you are on 0.5.6 or newer, the app updates itself. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** to **Applications**.
