# OkamiUNI 0.6.4 — Zoom, Teams and Zoho rooms

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.4/docs/releases/v0.6.4.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 15, 2026. App version **0.6.4**, build **19**.

## New

- **More meeting rooms.** Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Zoho Meeting now sit next to Google Meet in Settings › Calendars. Click **Connect**, sign in with your own account in the browser, and every new event can get its own room from that service.

## Fixes

- **Teams sign-in.** The Microsoft sign-in now returns to the app with the address Microsoft accepts.

## Installation

If you're on 0.5.6 or later, this version arrives inside the app. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** onto **Applications**.
