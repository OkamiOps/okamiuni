# OkamiUNI 0.6.1 — Unread at a glance

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.1/docs/releases/v0.6.1.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 15, 2026. App version **0.6.1**, build **16**.

## Fixes

- **Unread is easy to spot.** In the list, only an unread email has the purple dot, with sender and subject in bold. Read email is lighter. Before, every row had a dot in the account color and they all looked alike.
- **"All" is back in the sidebar**, right below Later, with the whole inbox.

## Installation

If you're on 0.5.6 or later, this version arrives inside the app. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** onto **Applications**.
