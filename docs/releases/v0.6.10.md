# OkamiUNI 0.6.10 — Formatted AI answers

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.10/docs/releases/v0.6.10.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 22, 2026. App version **0.6.10**, build **25**.

## What's new

- **The AI answer is formatted.** On an email, a line that is only a bold title becomes a title. A line that starts with a dash becomes a list. Bold and italic inside a sentence stay in the text.
- **The collapsed summary** shows the first sentence without asterisks.

## Installation

If you are on 0.5.6 or newer, the app updates itself. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** to **Applications**.
