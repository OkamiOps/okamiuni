# OkamiUNI 0.6.5 — Zoom for every account

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.5/docs/releases/v0.6.5.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 15, 2026. App version **0.6.5**, build **20**.

## What's new

- **Zoom ready for everyone.** OkamiUNI now uses its production app on the Zoom App Marketplace. Nothing changes for you: in Settings › Calendars, click **Connect** next to Zoom and sign in with your own account in the browser.

## Installation

If you are on 0.5.6 or newer, the app updates itself. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** to **Applications**.
