# OkamiUNI 0.6.0 — Glass

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.0/docs/releases/v0.6.0.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 15, 2026. App version **0.6.0**, build **15**.

## What's new

- **New look: Glass.** A rail on the left, a folder column and floating panels, in the Glass light and Glass night themes. Appearance also gains **Automatic**, which follows the Mac's light or dark mode.
- **All 26 classic themes** get the same layout, without the glass.
- **Day panel, Inbox and Calendar** redesigned. Inboxes from every account are unified, and accounts show as a tree.
- **A reader that fits.** The header is two lines and the email starts near the top. Everything AI lives in the **✦ AI** button (⌥⌘J), with its own shortcuts: ⌥⌘S summarize, ⌥⌘R draft reply, ⌥⌘E create event. The summary collapses to one line. The sender expands into the message details, with a button to copy each address.
- **Desk assistant.** The assistant shows answers, drafts and events as objects on a desk. When the desk is empty, it shows what can't wait in your day.
- **New Intelligence** settings. They show who does what and your connections. **Connect another AI** gathers every provider in one place: Apple Intelligence, Ollama, LM Studio, ChatGPT, Grok, Claude, MiniMax, Xiaomi MiMo, DeepSeek, Kimi, OpenRouter, OpenCode and agents. Grok signs in through its official login, with no API key.
- **Settings and windows** redesigned, with a sentence-based new appointment, the event window, accounts and confirmations.
- **New composer.** When you reply, the quote shows the original email formatted.

## Installation

Download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)**, open it and drag **OkamiUNI** onto **Applications**. If you're on 0.5.6 or later, this version arrives inside the app.

`OkamiUNI-0.6.0.zip` is the package used by automatic updates.
