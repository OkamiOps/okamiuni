# OkamiUNI 0.6.11 — The AI shows when it is writing

[Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/blob/v0.6.11/docs/releases/v0.6.11.pt-BR.md) · **English**

Released October 23, 2026. App version **0.6.11**, build **26**.

## What's new

- **You can see the AI working.** On an email, while it is writing, the previous answer is hidden. The card says it is writing and names who it is talking to.
- **The answer has a shape.** Summarize, tasks, terms and a question about the email come back with a title on its own line, a list, and a closing line. Titles in the card are larger than the body. A bold line becomes a title, and a dash line becomes a list.

## Installation

If you are on 0.5.6 or newer, the app updates itself. For a fresh install, download **[OkamiUNI.dmg](https://github.com/OkamiOps/okamiuni/releases/latest/download/OkamiUNI.dmg)** and drag **OkamiUNI** to **Applications**.
