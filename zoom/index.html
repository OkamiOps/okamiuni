OkamiUNI for Zoom

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Create a Zoom meeting for a calendar event right from OkamiUNI. The meeting is created in your own Zoom account and the join link goes into the event, so your guests receive it with the invitation.

Requirements

  • A Mac with macOS 26 or later.
  • A Zoom account (any plan).
  • OkamiUNI 0.6.4 or later.

Adding the app

  1. Download OkamiUNI, open the .dmg and drag OkamiUNI to Applications.
  2. Open OkamiUNI and go to Settings › Calendars › Meeting rooms.
  3. Click Connect next to Zoom. Your browser opens the Zoom sign-in page.
  4. Sign in to Zoom and click Allow. The browser shows a confirmation and you can close the tab.
  5. Back in OkamiUNI, Zoom now shows your Zoom account email.

Optional: set Default room in new events to Zoom, so every new event gets a Zoom meeting.

Using the app

  1. Open Calendar and click New event.
  2. Fill in the title, date and time.
  3. In the Meeting row, click change until it shows Zoom.
  4. Click Add. OkamiUNI creates a scheduled Zoom meeting with the event’s title, start time and duration, and saves the join link in the event.
  5. Open the event to see or copy the Zoom link. Guests you invite receive it with the invitation.

Removing the app

  1. In OkamiUNI, go to Settings › Calendars › Meeting rooms and click Disconnect next to Zoom. The Zoom tokens are deleted from your Mac.
  2. Sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace, click Manage › Added Apps, find OkamiUNI and click Remove.

After removal, OkamiUNI keeps no Zoom data. Meetings you already created stay in your Zoom account; delete them in Zoom if you no longer need them.

Data and permissions

OkamiUNI asks for meeting:write:meeting to create meetings you request and user:read:user to show your account email. Details in the Privacy Policy.
